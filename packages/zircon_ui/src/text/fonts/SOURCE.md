# Bundled regular fonts

Lilex Regular and IBM Plex Sans Regular, preserved with their SIL Open Font
Licenses, from Zed's assets at commit
`4a6121bb0f10c6e8037b741b7dc0a4cd639ae289`.

- Lilex: https://github.com/mishamyrt/Lilex
- IBM Plex: https://github.com/IBM/plex

The font bytes are embedded and loaded directly through CoreGraphics/CoreText.
They do not require installation, network access, or global font registration.
Only regular weights are bundled. System, Menlo and Monaco remain selectable.
